# Week 8 Resources

Recommended workflow:
- begin with physically interpretable features such as ring radius, azimuthal contrast, and low-order moments
- use simple baselines before moving to deep learning
- validate on synthetic data first, then transfer to experimental patterns cautiously

Questions to ask:
- which features are stable to detector noise and small beam shifts?
- can the classifier explain why two phases are separated?
- does the model generalize beyond the exact simulated conditions?
